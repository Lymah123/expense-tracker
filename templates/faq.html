Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new expense?
To add a new expense, click on "Add Expense" in the navigation menu. Fill in the amount, category, description, date, and select the appropriate currency, then click the "Add Expense" button.
Can I track recurring expenses?
Yes, you can set up recurring expenses by navigating to the "Recurring Expenses" section. There you can specify the amount, category, frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly), and the next due date.
How do I view reports of my spending?
Click on the "Reports" link in the navigation menu to access detailed reports and analytics about your spending patterns, categorized expenses, and monthly summaries.
Can I change the currency for my expenses?
Yes, when adding an expense, you can select the currency from the dropdown menu. You can manage available currencies in the "Currencies" section.
How do I attach receipts to my expenses?
Navigate to the "Receipts" section to upload and manage receipt images for your expenses. You can attach receipts to existing expenses or upload them while adding a new expense.
